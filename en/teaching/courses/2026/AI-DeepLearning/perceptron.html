The learning rule

z = w₁x₁ + w₂x₂ + b
ŷ = 1 if z ≥ 0 ; otherwise 0
e = y − ŷ
wᵢ ← wᵢ + η e xᵢ ; b ← b + η e

No update when the prediction is correct. One epoch is one pass over the 20 samples.
