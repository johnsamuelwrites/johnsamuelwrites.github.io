Dedication

In Honor and Gratitude

John Samuel

📐

Dedicated to my Art and Drawing Teachers who provided the foundational understanding of form, space, and visual representation. Your lessons about observing the world, understanding perspective, and expressing three-dimensional reality on two-dimensional surfaces laid the groundwork that made this spatial exploration possible.

🌱

Dedicated to those who embrace learning from zero—who approach new domains with curiosity rather than fear, who document their progress honestly, and who share their journey generously. Your courage to begin as beginners, to struggle visibly, and to grow publicly creates pathways for others to follow. Every model in this collection represents the accumulation of small steps from complete unfamiliarity toward growing understanding.

🔮

Dedicated to all who believe that spatial thinking can be learned, that three-dimensional modeling is not an innate gift but an acquirable skill, and that the journey from 2D to 3D representation—from flat projections to volumetric understanding—is open to anyone willing to invest time, attention, and persistent practice.
