References

Sources and Inspiration

John Samuel

Books

  • Perspective Made Easy, Ernest R. Norling — A foundational text on understanding perspective drawing, providing essential principles for representing three-dimensional space on two-dimensional surfaces.
  • Color, Betty Edwards — An exploration of color theory and its application in visual art, offering insights into how color perception influences spatial representation and three-dimensional modeling.

Articles & Resources

  • Blender — The primary open-source 3D modeling and rendering software used throughout Project 3D. Blender's comprehensive toolset enables creation, manipulation, and rendering of complex three-dimensional models.
  • Contextual Views — Understanding and manipulating Blender's viewport system, essential for effective 3D modeling workflow.
  • Making of Vertical Garden — Case study demonstrating professional 3D visualization techniques and workflows for creating realistic architectural renders.
  • How to emulate a Number Pad and 3 Button Mouse? — Technical solution for adapting Blender's interface to different hardware configurations, particularly useful for laptop users.
  • WebGL: 2D and 3D graphics for the web — Documentation on WebGL API, providing technical foundation for understanding web-based 3D rendering and the display of GLB models in browsers.
