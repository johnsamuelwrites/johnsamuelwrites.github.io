Project 3D

Exploring Dimensional Space Through Modeling

A comprehensive exploration of three-dimensional modeling and visualization techniques. This project documents the journey through spatial representation, from fundamental concepts to advanced 3D constructions, showcasing the intersection of mathematics, art, and computational geometry.
